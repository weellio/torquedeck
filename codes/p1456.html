P1456

EVAP leak – fuel tank side (Honda)

Manufacturer-specificHonda-specific code

What P1456 means

Your car's computer set P1456: EVAP leak – fuel tank side (Honda). It belongs to the manufacturer-specific group of codes. Manufacturers use codes in this range for their own meanings, so check that the description matches your car. The check engine light usually comes on once the fault has been seen on two drives in a row. Until then it shows up as a pending code.

Can I keep driving? Usually safe to drive. The car may run normally, but it will not pass an emissions test until this is fixed.

Common causes

  • Loose or worn gas cap
  • Leaking EVAP hose or valve
  • Failed fuel tank pressure sensor

Fix the cheapest, most likely cause first. Clearing the code without a repair only turns the light off until the fault comes back.

Check it yourself

Start with the gas cap. Then read the EVAP readiness monitor on the Car tab.
