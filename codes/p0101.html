P0101

MAF circuit range/performance

Fuel and air meteringGeneric code (all makes)

What P0101 means

Your car's computer set P0101: MAF circuit range/performance. It belongs to the fuel and air metering group of codes. It is a generic code, so it means the same thing on every make. The check engine light usually comes on once the fault has been seen on two drives in a row. Until then it shows up as a pending code.

Can I keep driving? Usually safe to drive for now, but get it checked soon. Left alone it can lower fuel economy, fail an emissions test or lead to a bigger repair.

Common causes

  • Dirty MAF sensor (often fixed with MAF cleaner)
  • Air leak between the MAF and the throttle body
  • Damaged wiring or connector
  • Failed MAF sensor

Fix the cheapest, most likely cause first. Clearing the code without a repair only turns the light off until the fault comes back.

Check it yourself

Graph MAF at idle and while revving. Readings that jump around or stay flat while RPM rises point to the sensor.
