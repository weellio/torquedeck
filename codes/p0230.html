P0230

Fuel pump primary circuit

Fuel and air meteringGeneric code (all makes)

What P0230 means

Your car's computer set P0230: Fuel pump primary circuit. It belongs to the fuel and air metering group of codes. It is a generic code, so it means the same thing on every make. The check engine light usually comes on once the fault has been seen on two drives in a row. Until then it shows up as a pending code.

Can I keep driving? Low fuel pressure can make the engine stall or hesitate. Get it checked before a long drive.

Common causes

  • Weak fuel pump
  • Clogged fuel filter
  • Faulty fuel pressure regulator
  • Low fuel level on a hot day

Fix the cheapest, most likely cause first. Clearing the code without a repair only turns the light off until the fault comes back.

Check it yourself

Fill the tank first. Watch fuel trims: they climb when the engine is starved of fuel.
