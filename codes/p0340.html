What P0340 means

Your car's computer set P0340: Camshaft position sensor A circuit (Bank 1). It belongs to the ignition system and misfire group of codes. It is a generic code, so it means the same thing on every make. The check engine light usually comes on once the fault has been seen on two drives in a row. Until then it shows up as a pending code.

Can I keep driving? Usually safe to drive for now, but get it checked soon. Left alone it can lower fuel economy, fail an emissions test or lead to a bigger repair.

Common causes

  • Failed camshaft position sensor
  • Wiring or connector problem
  • Timing chain stretch
  • Low or dirty oil affecting cam timing

Fix the cheapest, most likely cause first. Clearing the code without a repair only turns the light off until the fault comes back.

Check it yourself

Check the oil level. Look for timing codes (P0016–P0019) set at the same time.
