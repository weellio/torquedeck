P0300

Random/multiple cylinder misfire detected

Ignition system and misfireGeneric code (all makes)

What P0300 means

Your car's computer set P0300: Random/multiple cylinder misfire detected. It belongs to the ignition system and misfire group of codes. It is a generic code, so it means the same thing on every make. The check engine light usually comes on once the fault has been seen on two drives in a row. Until then it shows up as a pending code.

Can I keep driving? If the check engine light is flashing, slow down and stop driving soon. A bad misfire sends unburned fuel into the catalytic converter and can destroy it.

Common causes

  • Worn spark plugs or a failing ignition coil
  • Vacuum leak (cracked hose, intake gasket)
  • Low fuel pressure or dirty injectors
  • Low compression or a valve problem
  • Bad gas

Fix the cheapest, most likely cause first. Clearing the code without a repair only turns the light off until the fault comes back.

Check it yourself

Read the freeze frame to see RPM and load when it set. Watch fuel trims: high positive trims point to a vacuum leak or fuel delivery.
