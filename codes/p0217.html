P0217

Engine over-temperature condition

Fuel and air meteringGeneric code (all makes)

What P0217 means

Your car's computer set P0217: Engine over-temperature condition. It belongs to the fuel and air metering group of codes. It is a generic code, so it means the same thing on every make. The check engine light usually comes on once the fault has been seen on two drives in a row. Until then it shows up as a pending code.

Can I keep driving? Stop driving if the temperature gauge is high. An overheating engine can warp the head or blow the head gasket.

Common causes

  • Low coolant or a leak
  • Cooling fan not running
  • Stuck thermostat
  • Clogged radiator or failing water pump

Fix the cheapest, most likely cause first. Clearing the code without a repair only turns the light off until the fault comes back.

Check it yourself

Watch coolant temperature on the dashboard. Check the coolant level only once the engine is cold.
