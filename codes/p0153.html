P0153

O2 sensor slow response (B2S1)

Fuel and air meteringGeneric code (all makes)

What P0153 means

Your car's computer set P0153: O2 sensor slow response (B2S1). It belongs to the fuel and air metering group of codes. It is a generic code, so it means the same thing on every make. The check engine light usually comes on once the fault has been seen on two drives in a row. Until then it shows up as a pending code.

Can I keep driving? Usually safe to drive for now, but get it checked soon. Left alone it can lower fuel economy, fail an emissions test or lead to a bigger repair.

Common causes

  • Ageing or contaminated oxygen sensor
  • Exhaust leak in front of the sensor
  • Wiring or connector damage
  • A real fuel mixture problem the sensor is reporting

Fix the cheapest, most likely cause first. Clearing the code without a repair only turns the light off until the fault comes back.

Check it yourself

Graph the O2 sensor voltage and short-term fuel trim. A healthy upstream sensor switches quickly between about 0.1 and 0.9 V.
