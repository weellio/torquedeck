P2646

Rocker arm actuator A (VTEC) performance / stuck off

Engine computer and outputsGeneric code (all makes)

What P2646 means

Your car's computer set P2646: Rocker arm actuator A (VTEC) performance / stuck off. It belongs to the engine computer and outputs group of codes. It is a generic code, so it means the same thing on every make. The check engine light usually comes on once the fault has been seen on two drives in a row. Until then it shows up as a pending code.

Can I keep driving? Usually safe to drive for now, but get it checked soon. Left alone it can lower fuel economy, fail an emissions test or lead to a bigger repair.

Common causes

  • Low or dirty engine oil (VTEC runs on oil pressure)
  • Clogged VTEC solenoid screen or leaking gasket
  • Failed VTEC solenoid or oil pressure switch
  • Wiring or connector problem at the solenoid

Fix the cheapest, most likely cause first. Clearing the code without a repair only turns the light off until the fault comes back.

Check it yourself

Check the oil level first. Then watch RPM and load on the Graph tab while accelerating.
