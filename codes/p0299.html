P0299

Turbo/supercharger underboost

Fuel and air meteringGeneric code (all makes)

What P0299 means

Your car's computer set P0299: Turbo/supercharger underboost. It belongs to the fuel and air metering group of codes. It is a generic code, so it means the same thing on every make. The check engine light usually comes on once the fault has been seen on two drives in a row. Until then it shows up as a pending code.

Can I keep driving? Power will be low. Avoid hard acceleration until it is checked.

Common causes

  • Boost leak (cracked intercooler hose or loose clamp)
  • Sticking wastegate or boost control solenoid
  • Worn turbocharger

Fix the cheapest, most likely cause first. Clearing the code without a repair only turns the light off until the fault comes back.

Check it yourself

Graph boost (MAP) while accelerating to see whether it reaches normal levels.

Torque Deck reads P0299 with a cheap Bluetooth OBD2 adapter. It also shows the freeze frame (engine conditions at the moment the code set) and the live data you need to narrow it down.
