P0016

Crank/cam position correlation (Bank 1 Sensor A)

Fuel and air meteringGeneric code (all makes)

What P0016 means

Your car's computer set P0016: Crank/cam position correlation (Bank 1 Sensor A). It belongs to the fuel and air metering group of codes. It is a generic code, so it means the same thing on every make. The check engine light usually comes on once the fault has been seen on two drives in a row. Until then it shows up as a pending code.

Can I keep driving? Get this checked soon. A badly stretched chain can jump time and damage the engine.

Common causes

  • Stretched timing chain or worn guides
  • Timing belt or chain jumped a tooth
  • Faulty cam or crank position sensor
  • Variable valve timing solenoid stuck

Fix the cheapest, most likely cause first. Clearing the code without a repair only turns the light off until the fault comes back.

Check it yourself

A rattle from the front of the engine on cold start is a classic timing chain sign. Check the oil level and condition too.
