P2187

System too lean at idle (Bank 1)

Fuel and air meteringGeneric code (all makes)

What P2187 means

Your car's computer set P2187: System too lean at idle (Bank 1). It belongs to the fuel and air metering group of codes. It is a generic code, so it means the same thing on every make. The check engine light usually comes on once the fault has been seen on two drives in a row. Until then it shows up as a pending code.

Can I keep driving? Usually safe to drive for now, but get it checked soon. Left alone it can lower fuel economy, fail an emissions test or lead to a bigger repair.

Common causes

  • Vacuum leak (cracked hose, PCV, intake gasket)
  • Dirty or failing MAF sensor
  • Weak fuel pump or clogged fuel filter
  • Exhaust leak before the upstream O2 sensor

Fix the cheapest, most likely cause first. Clearing the code without a repair only turns the light off until the fault comes back.

Check it yourself

Watch long-term fuel trim. Above about +10% means the computer is adding a lot of fuel. If trim drops at higher RPM, a vacuum leak is likely.
