P0480

Cooling fan 1 control circuit

Emission controlsGeneric code (all makes)

What P0480 means

Your car's computer set P0480: Cooling fan 1 control circuit. It belongs to the emission controls group of codes. It is a generic code, so it means the same thing on every make. The check engine light usually comes on once the fault has been seen on two drives in a row. Until then it shows up as a pending code.

Can I keep driving? Watch the temperature gauge, especially in traffic. Stop if it climbs.

Common causes

  • Failed cooling fan motor
  • Bad fan relay or fuse
  • Wiring or connector problem

Fix the cheapest, most likely cause first. Clearing the code without a repair only turns the light off until the fault comes back.

Check it yourself

Watch coolant temperature at idle. The fan should switch on before about 225 °F (107 °C).
