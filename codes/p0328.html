P0328

Knock sensor 1 circuit high (Bank 1)

Ignition system and misfireGeneric code (all makes)

What P0328 means

Your car's computer set P0328: Knock sensor 1 circuit high (Bank 1). It belongs to the ignition system and misfire group of codes. It is a generic code, so it means the same thing on every make. The check engine light usually comes on once the fault has been seen on two drives in a row. Until then it shows up as a pending code.

Can I keep driving? Usually safe to drive for now, but get it checked soon. Left alone it can lower fuel economy, fail an emissions test or lead to a bigger repair.

Common causes

  • Failed knock sensor
  • Damaged wiring (often under the intake manifold)
  • Real engine knock from low-octane fuel or carbon build-up

Fix the cheapest, most likely cause first. Clearing the code without a repair only turns the light off until the fault comes back.

Check it yourself

Watch timing advance on the Graph tab. If it keeps dropping under load, the computer is pulling timing to protect the engine.
