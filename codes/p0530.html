P0530

A/C refrigerant pressure sensor circuit

Speed, idle and auxiliary inputsGeneric code (all makes)

What P0530 means

Your car's computer set P0530: A/C refrigerant pressure sensor circuit. It belongs to the speed, idle and auxiliary inputs group of codes. It is a generic code, so it means the same thing on every make. The check engine light usually comes on once the fault has been seen on two drives in a row. Until then it shows up as a pending code.

Can I keep driving? Usually safe to drive. The car may run normally, but it will not pass an emissions test until this is fixed.

Common causes

  • Low refrigerant
  • Failed A/C pressure sensor
  • Wiring or connector problem

Fix the cheapest, most likely cause first. Clearing the code without a repair only turns the light off until the fault comes back.

Check it yourself

The A/C may not blow cold. Safe to drive.
