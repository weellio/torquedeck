P0117

Coolant temp circuit low

Fuel and air meteringGeneric code (all makes)

What P0117 means

Your car's computer set P0117: Coolant temp circuit low. It belongs to the fuel and air metering group of codes. It is a generic code, so it means the same thing on every make. The check engine light usually comes on once the fault has been seen on two drives in a row. Until then it shows up as a pending code.

Can I keep driving? Usually safe to drive for now, but get it checked soon. Left alone it can lower fuel economy, fail an emissions test or lead to a bigger repair.

Common causes

  • Failed coolant temperature sensor
  • Damaged connector or wiring
  • Low coolant leaving the sensor in air

Fix the cheapest, most likely cause first. Clearing the code without a repair only turns the light off until the fault comes back.

Check it yourself

Compare coolant temperature with intake air temperature on a cold start. They should be close.
