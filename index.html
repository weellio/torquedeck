Raw commands · use at your own discretion

⚠ Use at your own discretion

The terminal sends raw commands straight to your car’s computers. That’s what makes customization possible, and also what makes it risky:

  • Write, reset or routine commands can change module settings, clear data, or leave a module unresponsive.
  • Wrong values can cause warning lights, limp mode, failed inspections or warranty problems.
  • Never send commands while driving. Do it parked, with the ignition on and a healthy battery.
  • Don’t use it to disable emissions or safety systems.
  • You are responsible for what you send. Torque Deck is provided as-is, with no warranty.
